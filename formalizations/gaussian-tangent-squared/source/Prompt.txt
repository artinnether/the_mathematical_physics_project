Gaussian integrals with squared shifted tangent poles

Let α>0 and b>0 be real numbers, set θ=arctan(b), D=1+b² and wα(x)=exp(−αx²), and define

q_b(x)=cos⁴(x)/(sin²(x)−b²cos²(x))².

Away from its poles this is 1/(tan²(x)−b²)², extended by zero across the tangent asymptotes; its double poles are kπ±θ for k∈ℤ, and arbitrary values at these poles do not affect the truncated integrals below.

For 0<δ<min(θ,π/2−θ), define

C_k(δ)=∫_{−π/2}^{−θ−δ} wα(kπ+t)q_b(kπ+t) dt
       +∫_{−θ+δ}^{θ−δ} wα(kπ+t)q_b(kπ+t) dt
       +∫_{θ+δ}^{π/2} wα(kπ+t)q_b(kπ+t) dt,

J_N(δ)=Σ_{k=−N}^{N}C_k(δ),

Mα(b)=Σ_{k∈ℤ}[wα(kπ+θ)+wα(kπ−θ)],

Lα(b)=Σ_{k∈ℤ}[−2α(kπ+θ)wα(kπ+θ)+2α(kπ−θ)wα(kπ−θ)]/D,

c_n(b)=(1+3b²)sin(2nθ)/b³−2n cos(2nθ)/b²   (n≥1).

Prove the following four parts in Lean.

(a) Prove absolute convergence of the series defining Mα(b) and Mα(b)>0; prove that J_N(δ) tends to a finite real value J(δ) as N→∞ for every admissible δ, and prove existence of the actual right-sided Hadamard finite-part limit

Hα(b)=lim_{δ→0+}[J(δ)−Mα(b)/(2b²D²δ)].

Prove also J(δ)→+∞ and δJ(δ)→Mα(b)/(2b²D²) as δ→0+.

(b) Prove absolute convergence of the Gaussian spectral series and evaluate the same finite part from (a):

Hα(b)=√(π/α)/D² [1+Σ_{n=1}^{∞}c_n(b)exp(−n²/α)].

For fixed α>0 define, for y>0,

Pα(y)=−√(π/α)/(1+y²) [1+(2/y)Σ_{n=1}^{∞}sin(2n arctan y)exp(−n²/α)].

Prove Pα′(b)=2bHα(b), prove absolute convergence of the series defining Lα(b), and prove Mα′(b)=Lα(b).

(c) For ε>0 define the actual whole-line complex Lebesgue integral

Fα,b(ε)=∫_ℝ wα(x)cos⁴(x)/[sin²(x)−b²cos²(x)+iεcos²(x)]² dx,

where i²=−1; prove absolute Lebesgue integrability for every ε>0 and prove

lim_{ε→0+}Fα,b(ε)=(Hα(b):ℂ)+iQα(b),

Qα(b)=π(1+3b²)Mα(b)/(4b³D²)−πLα(b)/(4b²D).

(d) For 0<r<1 define

K_b,r(x)=[1+Σ_{n=1}^{∞}rⁿc_n(b)cos(2nx)]/D²,

Bα,b(r)=∫_ℝ wα(x)K_b,r(x) dx.

Prove absolute Lebesgue integrability for every 0<r<1, prove the exact finite-r evaluation

Bα,b(r)=√(π/α)/D² [1+Σ_{n=1}^{∞}rⁿc_n(b)exp(−n²/α)],

and prove lim_{r→1−}Bα,b(r)=Hα(b) for the same actual finite part established in (a).

Use common witnesses J and H throughout; the convergence and identification claims must hold for the original integrals above, not quantities assigned the desired values by definition, and no required conclusion may be supplied as an additional hypothesis. Equivalent definitions and justified changes of variable are acceptable. Use real arithmetic in the coefficients, converting natural indices to ℝ before operations; indexing n≥1 by m+1 with m∈ℕ is acceptable.
